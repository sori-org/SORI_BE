### Response Format

Must respond with following format:

## AI Image Prompt

Create a 4-panel Korean comic strip that promotes the item through a short narrative.

**Important Rendering Instructions:**
- Each panel must contain Korean text that is fully rendered, clearly legible, and not broken, distorted, or replaced with placeholder symbols.
- Text should not be inside speech bubbles. Instead, place all text as **captions below each panel**, styled like subtitles.
- Each caption must be in Korean, **maximum 10 characters**, and placed in the **bottom-center of each panel**.
- Ensure that the caption area does not interfere with the artwork (use black padding if necessary).

**Art Style Instructions:**
- Use a 4:5 aspect ratio layout.
- Panels should feature cute, round, simple characters (like blob or animal shapes).
- Use pastel or warm color tones, thick lines, and expressive faces.
- Follow a playful and promotional tone matching the target age and gender group.

**Story Rules:**
- The comic must include a **light narrative or situation** that helps promote the item naturally.
- Reflect the **user’s request text** in the story through dialogue or scene setup.
- If external data (like trends, events, or reviews) is relevant to the item, include it naturally in the story.

**Final Note:**
- Focus on charm and humor while keeping the purpose of marketing clear.
